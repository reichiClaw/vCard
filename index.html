    Best way by device

    Safari on iPhone can open a vCard as a native contact sheet. Other platforms need a slightly different path.

    iPhone / iPad
    Visiting reichi.id serves the vCard directly — Safari opens the native contact sheet with no button press. To see this page on iOS instead, open /?page=1.
    Android
    Tap Add to Contacts. Chrome opens Android’s native contact editor pre-filled via an INSERT intent — no .vcf download. (Works in Chrome / most WebViews; other browsers may fall back to copying details.)
    macOS / Windows / Linux
    Download the .vcf for your desktop contacts app, or scan the QR with your phone to save it there (this is where QR actually helps).
