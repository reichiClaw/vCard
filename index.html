    Best way by device

    Every OS saves contacts differently. This page picks the smoothest path for yours automatically.

    iPhone / iPad

    Visiting reichi.id serves the vCard directly — Safari opens the native contact sheet with no button press. Prefer this page? Open /?page=1.

    Android

    Tap Add to Contacts. Chrome opens Android’s contact editor pre-filled via an INSERT intent — no .vcf download. Other browsers can copy the details instead.

    Desktop

    Download the .vcf for Contacts, People, or Outlook — or scan the QR with your phone to save it there instead.
